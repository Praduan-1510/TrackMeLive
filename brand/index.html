01 · The idea

A pin that talks.

On a map, a callout is the bubble that points at a place. In a conversation, it's the bubble that holds what someone says. Track Me Live is both at once: where you are, shared while you talk. So the mark is one shape that means both.

A map callout

Points at one exact place. The tail says "here".

A speech bubble

Someone is talking. The tail says "this is me speaking".

The Callout

One round, open shape: I'm here, and I'm on the phone with you. In the centre, the live ring and dot from the map.

  • One circle, one tail.A circle of radius 40 on a 128 grid. The tail leaves it between 205° and 250° and narrows to a softly rounded tip, down and to the left, like a pin's point.
  • Sides that curve in.Both sides of the tail are gently concave, so it feels like a gesture rather than a wedge.
  • The live centre.A ring of radius 21 and a dot of 10.5, both centred. It's the same ring and dot that mark a live location on the map.
  • No face, ever.The Callout is a place and a voice, not a character. The mascots are the characters.
02 · Logo

Three grounds, two lockups.

The horizontal lockup is the default. On the brand's amber, the Callout turns white and the i-dot follows. Use the stacked lockup in square spaces and the Callout alone where the name is already on screen.

xx

Clear space. Keep at least x on every side, where x is the width of the ring inside the Callout.

Minimum sizes. The Callout holds at 16px. Below 120px wide, drop the wordmark. Use the one-ink version for print and system tints.

03 · Live Together

When someone answers.

The Live Together mark is two callouts in conversation. Amber is you, blue is them, exactly as on the map. Use it for the feature itself: onboarding, the app stores, help pages. Everywhere else, the single Callout.

YouThe person sharing. Lamplight amber, tail down and to the left.
ThemThe person following, once they share back. Crossing blue, facing you.
/The gapA clear gap separates the two. They overlap; they never merge.
04 · Type

Friendly, but sure of itself.

Gabarito has round, open bowls and crisp ends: warm without looking childish. It sets the wordmark, titles and numbers. Figtree shares its geometry and stays clear at small sizes, so it does the reading on Android and in every browser but Safari. On iPhone and in Safari, body text stays in SF Pro.

06 · App icon

A warm square on the home screen.

The default icon is a white Callout on amber: easy to find and friendly at a glance. Ship it as layers in Icon Composer so iOS 27 adds Liquid Glass, with dark and tinted appearances. On Android, the same Callout ships as adaptive layers and a one-ink themed layer.

iPhoneIcon Composer layers, Liquid Glass

AndroidAdaptive layers; the device picks the mask

  • Adaptive iconBackground and foreground layers, 108 by 108dp. The device masks them to 72dp; the Callout stays inside the 66dp circle.
  • Themed iconOne ink on the same 108dp grid. Android uses only the shape and tints it.
  • Google Play512 by 512px, 32-bit PNG, full bleed, no shadow or corners. Play adds the mask.
07 · Mascots

The characters live on the map.

The Callout stays simple so the mascots can be the personalities. Twelve characters in three sets mark people on the map and star in onboarding. One studio, one camera and one lighting setup, so they always read as a set. A color and, where it fits, an accessory make each one personal.

ClassicsSoft vinyl creatures and kit

AnimePrinted eyes, sculpted hair

Movie nightSculpted eyes, real tailoring

Color changes fur, hair or clothes, never skin. Each of the five picker colors re-renders the Classics' fur, body or shell, the anime hair or the movie-night garment. Skin, eyes, visors and lamps stay as they are.

Every character is an original. Never model a real actor or someone else's character, however loved. Borrow the genre, not the face.

AccessoriesThree extras, each on its own layer

Accessories keep their own colors. They're drawn as a layer over the mascot and never tinted, so every color works with every accessory.

Only where they fit. No glasses over aviators, nothing on a helmet. Each character takes only the ones that fit, and the astronaut takes none.

Two images, one box. Lay accessories/<id>-<accessory>.png over the render in the same square. Both come from the same camera, so they line up at any size.

11 · Files

Everything is in brand/logo/.

Every file is SVG, built by brand/tools/build-logo.mjs from one geometry and the Gabarito outlines. The one PNG, the Google Play icon, is exported from its SVG by brand/tools/export-play-png.mjs. Change the scripts, never the files.
